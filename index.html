Meet Our Expert

Our Ayurvedic Doctors

Certified Ayurvedic Physician

Dr. Jyoti Thakur

Chief Physician & Founder

Dr. Jyoti Thakur is a dedicated Ayurvedic physician and holistic healthcare practitioner with over a decade of clinical experience. She earned her Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) from the esteemed Institute of Medicine (IOM), building a strong foundation in classical Ayurvedic principles and their application in modern clinical practice.

With a patient-centered approach, Dr. Thakur integrates the wisdom of traditional Ayurveda with contemporary medical understanding to provide personalized, comprehensive care. Her clinical expertise includes chronic health conditions, digestive disorders, lifestyle-related concerns, stress management, Panchakarma, and preventive healthcare.

A particular area of her expertise is Nadi Pariksha (Ayurvedic pulse examination)—a traditional diagnostic approach through which she assesses an individual's Prakriti (constitutional nature) and Vikriti (current imbalances). By carefully understanding each patient's unique constitution and health patterns, she develops individualized treatment strategies aimed at restoring balance and supporting long-term well-being.

Her approach goes beyond treating symptoms; she focuses on understanding the root causes of imbalance, lifestyle, diet, constitution, and individual needs of each patient. Through personalized Ayurvedic therapies, dietary guidance, lifestyle recommendations, and therapeutic interventions, Dr. Thakur strives to help her patients achieve sustainable health and harmony.

"Every individual is unique, and Ayurveda works best when treatment is personalized to that uniqueness."

Featured Ayurveda Surgeon

Dr. Dikshya Paudel

Featured at Annapurna Ayurveda | Consultant Ayurveda Surgeon

Dr. Dikshya Paudel is Nepal's first female Ayurveda surgeon, specializing in Shalya Tantra and clinical surgical care.

  • Shalyakarma, Kshar karma: Anorectal disorders - piles, fissure-in-ano, fistula-in-ano, pilonidal sinus
  • Urological cases: Ureteric and renal calculi, benign prostate hyperplasia, urethral stricture
  • Skin and soft tissue: Keloid, lipoma, warts, skin tags
  • Jalaukavacharan: Non-healing ulcers, varicose vein
  • Orthopaedic cases: Marmin chikitsa, Agnikarma, Viddha karma, Raktamokshan for osteoarthritis, frozen shoulder, lumbar spondylosis, tennis elbow, plantar fasciitis, and post-traumatic stiffness

Availability: Dr. Dikshya Paudel is available for consultation on weekends only, and prior pre-booking is required.

"Focused care, surgical precision, and holistic healing guide every stage of recovery."

Gynecology & Obstetrics Specialist

Dr. Bihani Sanjyal

Consultant in Ayurveda | Gynecology & Obstetrics

Dr. Bihani Sanjyal is a Consultant in Ayurveda with a specialization in Gynecology and Obstetrics. She completed her BAMS in 2018 from Ayurveda Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal, and earned her MS degree in Ayurveda Gynecology and Obstetrics from the National Institute of Ayurveda, India, in 2023.

She has also completed the Yoga Instructor Course from SVYASA, India, and has been actively practicing across reputed Ayurveda centres in Nepal. Her work focuses on holistic reproductive health, menstrual wellness, hormonal balance, preconception care, pregnancy wellness, and menopause wellness.

She specializes in managing complex conditions through classical Ayurvedic therapies and lifestyle interventions:

  • PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)
  • Infertility
  • Tubal blockage
  • Abnormal uterine bleeding

Availability: Dr. Bihani Sanjyal is available once a month by prior appointment, and pre-booking is required.

"Dedicated to women’s health through classical Ayurveda, lifestyle guidance, and compassionate care."
